## Phase 2: Investigation

### Reinforcement Learning Basics
**Positive Reinforcement**: Adding something desirable after a behavior to increase its likelihood
- Example: Praise after correct performance
- Mechanism: Behavior → Positive outcome → Increased behavior frequency

**Negative Reinforcement**: Removing something undesirable after a behavior to increase its likelihood  
- Example: Removing restrictions after compliance
- Mechanism: Behavior → Removal of negative stimulus → Increased behavior frequency

**Key Distinction**: Both increase behavior frequency, but through different mechanisms (adding good vs. removing bad)
