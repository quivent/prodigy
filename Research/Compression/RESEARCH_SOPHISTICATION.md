## Phase 2: Investigation

### Sophistication Definition and Components

**Sophistication**: Advanced development characterized by complexity, refinement, and nuanced understanding that goes beyond simple or basic approaches.

**Key Elements**:
- **Complexity Handling**: Managing multiple interrelated factors simultaneously
- **Nuanced Understanding**: Recognizing subtle distinctions and relationships
- **Refined Execution**: Elegant, efficient solutions that demonstrate mastery
- **Strategic Thinking**: Multi-level planning with awareness of implications
- **Autonomous Judgment**: Independent decision-making based on principles

### Sophistication Indicators
- **Integration**: Combining multiple concepts into coherent wholes
- **Meta-Awareness**: Understanding systems at multiple levels of abstraction
- **Anticipation**: Predicting needs and consequences before they occur
- **Optimization**: Finding elegant solutions that serve multiple purposes
- **Self-Correction**: Identifying and fixing problems independently
