## Phase 2: Investigation

### Analysis Definition and Types

**Analysis**: The detailed examination of elements or structure of something, breaking it down into components to understand the whole.

**Types of Analysis**:
- **Decomposition**: Breaking complex systems into component parts
- **Pattern Recognition**: Identifying recurring themes or structures
- **Causal Analysis**: Understanding cause-and-effect relationships
- **Comparative Analysis**: Examining similarities and differences
- **Systems Analysis**: Understanding how parts interact within wholes
- **Critical Analysis**: Evaluating strengths, weaknesses, and validity

### Analytical Methods

**Structural Analysis**: 
- Identify components and their relationships
- Map dependencies and hierarchies
- Assess completeness and gaps

**Functional Analysis**:
- Examine what each component does
- Assess how well functions are performed
- Identify redundancies and inefficiencies

**Risk Analysis**:
- Identify potential failure points
- Assess probability and impact of failures
- Evaluate mitigation strategies

**Gap Analysis**:
- Compare current state to desired state
- Identify missing elements or capabilities
- Prioritize improvements needed
